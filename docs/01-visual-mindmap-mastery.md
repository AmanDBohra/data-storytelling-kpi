## 0. THE ONE IDEA ABOVE ALL
```
BUSINESS GOAL → BUSINESS QUESTION → KPI → DATA → ANALYSIS →
INSIGHT → STORY → DECISION → ACTION → OUTCOME → NEW KPI
```
```
KPI = WHAT is happening · ANALYSIS = WHY · STORYTELLING = SO WHAT · RECOMMENDATION = NOW WHAT
```
**Memory hook:** *WHAT · WHY · SO WHAT · NOW WHAT.*

## 1. THE MASTER MIND MAP (recreate from memory)
```
BUSINESS GOAL → KEY QUESTIONS → KPIs
  ├ TARGET · ACTUAL · TREND
  ↓ VARIANCE   ← WHAT
  ↓ ANALYSIS   ← WHY
  ↓ INSIGHT
  ↓ STORY      ← SO WHAT
  ↓ DECISION   ← NOW WHAT
  ↓ ACTION (owner + date)
  ↓ IMPACT → new KPI
```

## 2. THE MOST IMPORTANT QUESTION
> **"What decision will this information help someone make?"** No decision → why are we measuring it? This kills vanity metrics on contact.

## 3. ONE SENTENCE EACH
Storytelling: turning analysis into a narrative — what happened, why it matters, what to do. `DATA → MEANING → MESSAGE → ACTION`.
KPI design: the right measurable indicators of whether an objective is met. `GOAL → MEASURE → TARGET → STATUS → ACTION`.

## 4. VANITY vs ACTIONABLE
```
VANITY: Website Visits → "so what decision?" → none
ACTIONABLE: Conversion Rate → improve page/pricing/funnel/campaign
```
A metric becomes valuable only when it connects to a decision. Pair vanity totals with a rate or a cohort, or drop them.

## 5. KPI HIERARCHY
```
BUSINESS GOAL → STRATEGIC KPI → DRIVER KPI → OPERATIONAL KPI → ACTIVITIES
```
Strategy watched monthly; operations daily.

## 6. KPI TREE — what DRIVES a KPI
```
PROFIT = REVENUE − COST
REVENUE = PRICE × VOLUME ;  VOLUME = ORDERS → CUSTOMERS
```
E-commerce identity: **Revenue = Traffic × Conversion × AOV.** Any revenue move lives in one of those three branches. Tree answers *why it moved*; scorecard answers *how are we doing*.

## 7. KPI = NUMBER + CONTEXT
```
Revenue = ₹100M → "compared with what?" vs Target · LY · LM · Budget · Forecast · Benchmark · Peer
```

## 8. KPI MASTER FORMULA
```
KPI = VALUE + TARGET + TIME + COMPARISON + TREND + THRESHOLD + OWNER + ACTION
Revenue ₹125M · Target ₹120M · +4.2% · YoY +8% · ↑ · Owner: Sales · Action: maintain
```

## 9. KPI DEFINITION TEMPLATE (never vague)
```
Name · Definition · Formula · Numerator · Denominator · Unit · Grain · Time Period ·
Data Source · Target · Benchmark · Owner · Frequency · Threshold · Direction · Action
```
🎯 **The denominator is where KPIs quietly lie.** State it explicitly every time.

## 10. FORMULA THINKING (derive, don't memorize)
"What % of customers return?" → Returning ÷ Eligible. Margin = profit ÷ revenue = a *keep-rate* (for every ₹100 of sales, how much we keep).

## 11. LEADING vs LAGGING
```
LEADING = warning (pipeline, engagement, trial activation, complaints)
LAGGING = result (revenue, profit, churn)
```
*Leading steers, lagging scores.* A deck of only lagging KPIs is a rear-view mirror.

## 12. INPUT → PROCESS → OUTPUT → OUTCOME
`Leads → Qualified → Conversions → Revenue`. Orgs over-watch outcomes, ignore the drivers they can move.

## 13. BALANCED DESIGN & TRADE-OFFS
Revenue ↑ but Margin ↓; Conversion ↑ but Refunds ↑; Calls/day ↑ but CSAT ↓. **Measure the outcome AND its guardrails.**

## 14. GUARDRAILS (prevent gaming)
`PRIMARY KPI + GUARDRAIL KPI` (Conversion ↑ + Refund rate must not rise). People optimize the metric, not the business — pair every incentive KPI with a quality guardrail.

## 15. KPI CASCADE
`COMPANY → BUSINESS UNIT → DEPARTMENT → TEAM → INDIVIDUAL`. Every lower KPI ladders up to a business outcome.

## 16. OWNERSHIP & THRESHOLDS
`KPI → OWNER → TARGET → REVIEW FREQUENCY → ACTION`. GREEN on track · AMBER attention · RED action. Business-defined, not arbitrary colors.

## 17. NORTH STAR
The single metric capturing core value (Northwind: *monthly repeat-purchasing customers*). Don't copy another company's — it encodes their value.

## 18. NUMERACY TRAPS
Absolute vs relative (state the base) · percentage **point** vs percent (20%→25% = +5 pts AND +25% relative) · rate vs count · average vs median (mean lies with an outlier). Pick the stat from the *question*.

## 19. STORYTELLING MASTER MAP
```
DATA → OBSERVATION → PATTERN → INSIGHT → IMPLICATION → RECOMMENDATION → ACTION
```
**DATA ≠ INSIGHT:** "Sales fell 12%" (data) vs "the drop is concentrated in the West, where Product A fell 25%" (insight).

## 20. TWO TESTS + WHY CHAIN
`SO WHAT?` forces meaning · `WHY?` forces cause. Chain: WHAT HAPPENED → WHY → WHAT CAUSED IT → SO WHAT → NOW WHAT.

## 21. THE EXECUTIVE STORY
```
HEADLINE · EVIDENCE · DRIVER · IMPACT · RECOMMENDATION · ACTION
"Revenue is 8% below target. Three straight months below plan. West = 70% of the gap.
Quarter target at risk. Investigate West churn + Product A availability. Regional intervention in 2 weeks."
```

## 22. HEADLINE-FIRST + PYRAMID
```
BAD title: "Monthly Sales Analysis"
GOOD title: "Sales are 8% below target, driven by West-region weakness."
```
Start with the answer, then prove it, then detail.

## 23. STORY STRUCTURE & TYPES
`CONTEXT → PROBLEM → EVIDENCE → DRIVERS → IMPACT → RECOMMENDATION → ACTION`. Seven patterns: Performance · Trend · Driver · Comparison · Exception · Opportunity · Risk.

## 24. CHART SELECTION (ask the question)
Trend→line · Compare→bar · Rank→horizontal bar · Part-of-whole→stacked (donut 2–3) · Distribution→histogram/box · Relationship→scatter · Actual vs Target→bullet · KPI→card · Geography→map · Detail→table. *Ask "what relationship must I show?"*

## 25. DATA-INK, COLOR, HIERARCHY
Remove non-meaning ink (3D, heavy gridlines, rainbow). gray=context, blue=normal, green=positive, red=risk, amber=attention; one accent; never color alone. Biggest emphasis = most important — *"what should they notice first?"*

## 26. DASHBOARD FLOW + TESTS
```
HEADLINE / KPIs → TREND → DRIVERS | EXCEPTIONS → RECOMMENDATION / ACTION
```
30-second test (what's happening & what matters?) and 5-minute test (drill to root cause?). A dashboard that stops at LOOK (never DECIDE→ACT) is just a report.

## 27. AUDIENCE-FIRST
`WHO? → CARE ABOUT? → DECISION? → INFO? → ACTION?` CEO=impact/risk/decision · CFO=revenue/margin/variance · Sales=pipeline/conversion/quota · Ops=volume/SLA/efficiency · Analyst=detail/drivers/root cause.

## 28. ANALYSIS TOOLKIT
Root cause: `SYMPTOM → PATTERN → DRIVER → ROOT CAUSE`. Correlation ≠ causation (check mechanism, order, confounders). Hypothesis-driven. Exception analysis (top/bottom/outliers/change). Pareto 80/20. **Contribution analysis** and the **variance/waterfall bridge** (break a gap into additive drivers) — the single most persuasive executive artifact.

## 29. NUMBER → MEANING
`NUMBER → CHANGE → SIGNIFICANCE → BUSINESS IMPACT` (Churn 7% → +2 pts → largest rise in 12 months → ~₹5M at risk). Compress numbers into a sentence, then show the chart.

## 30. WORKSHOP CANVASES
**KPI canvas:** goal · success means · primary KPI · driver KPIs · leading KPIs · guardrails · target · owner · review freq · red threshold · action.
**Story canvas:** WHAT · WHY · SO WHAT · NOW WHAT · who acts · by when.

## 31. EXECUTIVE DELIVERY
One-slide: HEADLINE → KPI/TREND/TARGET → WHY (2–3 drivers) → SO WHAT (impact) → NOW WHAT (rec + owner + date). Speaking template: *"The headline is X. Driven by Y. Impact is Z. We recommend A because B. Next action is C, owned by D, by [date]."*

## 32. FUNCTION KPI LIBRARY
Sales · Marketing · Customer Success · Finance · Operations · HR — see the course doc for the full lists.

## 33. ANTI-PATTERNS
Vanity · ambiguous · too many · no owner/target/comparison · wrong denominator · lagging-only · conflicting · gameable.

## 34–35. CHECKLISTS & 10 GOLDEN QUESTIONS
Story checklist (headline, audience, KPI defs, validated numbers, comparison, drivers, root cause, impact, recommendation, owner, timeline). KPI checklist (relevant, measurable, defined, unambiguous, target, owner, comparable, timely, actionable, guardrail).
```
1 goal 2 decision 3 which KPI 4 vs what 5 what changed 6 why 7 impact 8 cost of nothing 9 what do 10 how know it worked
```

# CAPSTONE — "WHY IS PROFIT DECLINING?"
Revenue *growing*, profit suspected deteriorating. Scorecard: Revenue 110M🟢, Profit 18M vs 22M🔴, Margin 16.4% vs 21%🔴, Discount 9% vs 6%🔴, Retention 92%🟢. Decompose profit → revenue +10% but profit −5% because discounts rose and mix shifted to low-margin SKUs → margin compressed ~4.6 pts. **Headline:** "Revenue grew 10% but profit fell 5% — growth was *bought* via discounting, not earned. Review discount policy and low-margin SKUs; protect margin, not just sales." Present in 5 min.

# THE SKEPTICAL-CEO GAUNTLET
Be ready for: "Why care? How do you know? What's causing it? What if we do nothing? What exactly do you recommend? How much impact? Who owns it? When will we know it worked?" Simulate from every seat.
